## Notes
* The component renders a two-column hero with text content and image.
* `image_position` supports `right` and `left`.
* `align` supports `text-start` and `text-center`.
* Overlay is only visible when a background image is present and `background_overlay_color` is not `bg-none`.
* Button renders only when both `button_label` and `button_url` are provided.

  align: 'text-start',
